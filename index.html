Who We Are

What is TechBridge?

We are a purpose built training center that closes the gap between academic learning and the skills employers actually need.

A bridge from learning to earning

TechBridge was founded to give students and early career professionals a clear, structured path into technology roles. We combine project based training, industry mentorship, career support, and a focused 30 day practical internship in one place.

Whether you're switching careers, strengthening your portfolio, or preparing for your first tech job, we provide the tools, community, and guidance to get you there.

Outcome focused curriculum

Every program is designed around real job requirements and portfolio projects.

Mentorship from industry

Learn from engineers, designers, and product leaders who work at top companies.
